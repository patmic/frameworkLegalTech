Dear Reviewer,

Thank you very much for taking the time to review this manuscript. We are
grateful for the encouraging assessment and, in particular, for the structural
observations: separating the discussion from the conclusions and giving the
policy material a section of its own made the argument considerably easier to
follow, and the abstract is a genuine improvement on what we submitted.

All nine points raised in your report have been addressed. Revised passages are
marked in blue in the re-submitted manuscript, and no scientific content,
numerical result or claim has changed in this revision.

A detailed point-by-point response, following the journal template and giving
the section and page number for each change, is provided in the attachment.

Thank you again for the care you gave the manuscript.

Yours sincerely,
Patricio M. Paccha-Angamarca, on behalf of all authors
